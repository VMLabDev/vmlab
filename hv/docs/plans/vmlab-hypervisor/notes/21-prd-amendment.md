# PRD amendment

## Question
When and how is the §1.2 non-goal amended?

## Answer
When the hypervisor selector lands in vmlab, together with an ADR.

## Why
The PRD binds what vmlab does, and the selector is the first change a vmlab user can see. Recommended option, accepted.

## Consequences
None beyond those already on the map.
