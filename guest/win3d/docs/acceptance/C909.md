# C909. Direct3D 11 shaders with more than 32 combined texture slots

Draft D4 of the acceptance pass of card #886
([acceptance-2026-11.md](../../../../docs/agents/acceptance-2026-11.md#d4-win3d-direct3d-11-pixel-shaders-with-more-than-32-combined-texture-slots-draw-nothing)):
Wine's `test_high_resource_count` failed its two checks, `d3d11.c:36343`
and `d3d11.c:36355`, on both templates. [C375](C375.md) traced the cause.
The UMD gives each texture and sampler pair of a shader, and each typed
buffer load, one combined slot. It had 32 slots, the shader needs 150, and
`CreatePixelShader` replaced it with a shader that writes zero.

The fix is
[`d3d10umd-combined-texture-slots-above-32.patch`](../../patches/mesa/d3d10umd-combined-texture-slots-above-32.patch),
the last patch of the Mesa series. The driver with it is `100.6.101.58123`,
package `0.1.3` built at `2dc9e44b`. The sha256 of the x64 UMD
`viogpu_d3d10_x64.dll` is `724c0c8a…`, of the x86 UMD `3e8a290f…`.

## The change

The combined slot range has `PIPE_MAX_SHADER_SAMPLER_SLOTS` entries, 192.
The 32 came from five places:

| Place | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ShaderTGSI.c` and `Shader.cpp`, the slot table and the sampler binding map | 32 entries, 32-bit masks | 192 entries, masks of six words |
| TGSI (`tgsi_ureg.c`, `tgsi_scan.c`, `tgsi_to_nir.c`) | Sampler and sampler-view registers up to 32 | Up to 192. The 32-bit masks of `tgsi_shader_info` and the bitsets of `shader_info` keep their size and have no bit for a higher register |
| `nir_to_spirv.c` | A table of 32 sampler variables | 192 |
| Yttrium shader state | Used slots taken from `shader_info` | `struct yttrium_sampler_slot_mask`, filled from the tokens and the sampler variables |
| Yttrium pipeline | 32 sampled images | 192. Slots 0 to 31 keep their descriptor bindings. Slots 32 and up get a second binding range behind the storage images |

A shader that uses at most 32 slots therefore compiles to the same SPIR-V
as before.

## Costs

Three per-draw costs would have grown six times with the arrays. Each is
bounded by the number of sampled bindings the pipeline uses:

- The pipeline key is cleared and compared up to that number.
- A deferred draw record is cleared up to it. Its four sampled arrays are
  at the end of the record for this reason. The record itself is larger by
  their size.
- The search for a sampler object stops at it.

No draw rate was measured. The adapter checks that time frames (`pace`,
`tear-flip`, `tear-blt`, `latency`) pass.

A shader that needs more than 192 slots still gets the zero shader, and the
log line names 192. The pipeline layout of a shader with more than 32
slots is not checked against the host's
`maxPerStageDescriptorSampledImages`. RADV on AMD1 accepted 150. Nvidia2
was not run.
