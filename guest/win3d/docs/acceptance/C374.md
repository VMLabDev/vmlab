# C374. Direct3D 11 NV12 textures

The [C328 Wine baseline](C328.md) had 13 Direct3D 11 failures. Eleven came
from `test_nv12`, where every valid even-sized texture returned
`E_OUTOFMEMORY`. Windows advertised NV12 texture creation, but Mesa's
D3D11 frontend had no backing format for the allocation. The test reached
its view, render, and copy checks only after creation worked.

The [Mesa patch](../../patches/mesa/d3d10umd-back-nv12-with-two-texture-planes.patch)
stores luma in a full-size `R8_UINT` image and interleaved chroma in a
half-width, half-height `R8G8_UINT` image. Typed shader and render-target
views select their plane by format and keep that plane at bind time.
Initial data, updates, copies and staging maps preserve the two-plane
layout. Copies and updates with odd luma coordinates or dimensions leave
the destination alone.

Wine 11.0 ran with `WINETEST_NO_MT_D3D=1` on helios with Venus and
the vmlab-hv bundle launcher. The guests were private copies of the
provisioned Windows 11 `26100.1742.11#win3d` acceptance guests from
C356. The signed Win3D 0.1.3 bundle rebuilt Mesa for x64 and x86 at
`c8b050a4` and reused matching pinned outputs for the other components.
The installed x64 DLL matched the signed bundle on both guests.

The eleven NV12 creation failures are gone. Wine exercised NV12
typed shader and render-target views, rendering, updates, copies and
staging readback with zero failures. The remaining D3D11 failures match
the two C328 `test_high_resource_count` assertions
(`d3d11.c:36343` and `d3d11.c:36355`). Both Wine programs reported
the same skip messages as in C328. The Nvidia2 monitor saw no Xid events.

The raw outputs, DLL hash comparisons and shutdown logs are in
`Nvidia2-final/` and `AMD1-final/` under
`/home/vmlab/src/stc/card-374-work/` on helios.
