# C938. The torn frame of `tear-blt` on windows-10 is a race of two guest copies

Card #938 of the vmlab board. Draft D8 of
[acceptance-2026-11.md](../../../../docs/agents/acceptance-2026-11.md#d8-win3d-one-torn-frame-in-tear-blt-on-nvidia2-windows-10):
the adapter row `tear-blt` showed one torn frame of about 1260 in 2 of 4
runs on `windows-10` on Nvidia2.

The cause is found and it is not in the driver. Windows keeps two more
copies of the window in guest RAM behind the destination of the Blt present,
and it makes both with guest CPUs. When the probe presents twice inside one
tick, Windows holds the first of these copies for the second present until
the next vsync report. DWM starts the second copy on the same report. The
two copies then run at the same time, and DWM takes the upper rows of the
new frame and the lower rows of the frame before. The destination that the
driver's host copy writes ([present-copy.md](../present-copy.md)) is whole
in every torn frame. No change to the driver or to vmlab-hv was made.

## The path of a frame

The probe (`tests/e2e/adapter/probes/tear_probe.cpp`, `--mode blt`) fills
its 800x480 window with one colour of eight, the next colour on each frame,
through a blt-model DXGI swap chain at interval one. A frame reaches the
screen in four copies:

1. The present copy. The kernel driver sends
   `VIRTIO_GPU_CMD_VMLAB_PRESENT_COPY`, and vmlab-hv copies the back buffer
   into the destination D, a resource with a guest backing. It takes 60 to
   140 us, and the source digest is one colour every time.
2. D to A, on a guest CPU, about 2 ms later.
3. A to B, on a guest CPU, just before DWM's submission.
4. DWM's composition into the scanout, and the flip.

Copies 2 and 3 are not made by vmlab-hv, by the kernel driver or by a
command of the control queue: D is attached to no context and is never
transferred, and no host command falls between the present copy and the
change of A. Which Windows component makes each was not identified. The
host sees only the guest pages. B is called DWM's copy here because it
changes 0.3 to 0.5 ms before each ring-1 submission of DWM's context and
the published frame shows what B held.

## A torn frame

From `ev/poll1/run-2` (`bin/seq2.py`, the second torn frame), times in ms,
0 is the first poll that shows the two copies running. Colours: Y yellow,
M magenta, C cyan. The numbers are pages.

```text
 -24.30  PRESENT COPY               D = M
 -22.47      A = M375               on time, 1.8 ms after the copy
 -18.47  PRESENT COPY               D = C, 5.8 ms after the present before
 -17.86  SET_SCANOUT_BLOB           the flip of DWM's frame before
  +0.00      A = M190 C185          the held copy D to A runs, 18.5 ms late
  +0.00      B = Y230 C144          DWM's copy A to B runs with it
  +0.16      A = C375
  +0.16      B = C193 M181          cyan above, magenta below
  +0.57  DWM submission
 +26.50  publish C249,M231          the torn frame, rows of each colour
```

The copy into B starts behind the copy into A and overtakes it: the pages
it reads first are already cyan, the rest still magenta. That is the shape of
every torn frame of this card and of D8: the upper part is one frame newer
than the lower part, and the split is at any row.

The second present of the pair comes 4 to 6 ms after the first because the
first was late and the probe renders the next frame at once. Windows does
not copy that second frame into A until the next vsync report. The kernel
driver raises the report when the flip is on the host, and DWM, which
waits for its flip, starts its next composition on the same report. In the
first torn frame of the run the fence of the ordered flip was done 40 us
before the first poll that shows both copies. So the two copies start
within about 100 us of each other, and each takes about 150 us.

## What does not cause it

The earlier holds of the card ruled these out. Each is a hold of 2 to 10
runs with a diagnostic vmlab-hv (`bin/diag1.patch` to `diag11.patch`, not in
the repository).

| Code | Test | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T1 | A stall of 4 ms at half of every host present copy | 5 torn of 10,800 frames, splits at rows 402 to 473, none at row 240: no reader runs during the host copy (`ev/stall1`) |
| T2 | `clflush` of the destination after the copy | More torn frames, 1 to 71 per run of 1,800: not stale host cache lines (`ev/flush1`) |
| T3 | The host copy bottom-up | The new colour stays on top: not the host's write order (`ev/rev1`) |
| T4 | D and every mapped blob sampled at each DWM submission and `SET_SCANOUT_BLOB` | D is one colour each time. DWM's 16 MiB upload blob never holds the window (`ev/snap1` to `ev/snap6`) |

T1, T2 and the 100 us poll of F3 raise the rate (2 torn of about 890 frames
in each run of `ev/poll1`). They slow the guest's copies or move the phase
of the present, so two presents fall inside one tick more often.

## Why windows-10 on Nvidia2

Not measured. The race needs a late frame followed by one inside the same
tick. `windows-11` on Nvidia2 and both templates on AMD1 showed 0 torn
frames in the runs of card #916, which is at most 4 runs of 1260 frames
each, and the rate here is 1 in 12,000. Those runs do not show that the
other three are free of it.

## What is left

- The row `tear-blt` stays as it is. It will fail again on `windows-10` at
  about this rate, with one frame whose upper part is one frame newer than
  the lower part. That frame is this race and not a fault of the present
  copy or of the flip.
- A fix in the driver would have to take the two guest copies away: a
  destination that DWM samples on the GPU, which means GDI-compatible
  surfaces in the kernel and user-mode drivers. That is new work and not
  part of this card.
- Not done: naming the Windows components that make the two copies (a
  guest-side trace), and a rate for `windows-11` and for AMD1 over as many
  frames as F1.
